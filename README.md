# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is an American sci-fi horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, it centers around the disappearance of a young boy and the appearance of a girl with psychokinetic abilities.

## Why I Love It
* **Nostalgic 80s Aesthetic:** Great soundtrack, retro fashion, and classic pop-culture references.
* **Compelling Characters:** Strong friendships and great character development across seasons.
* **Mystery & Suspense:** Keeps you guessing with every episode.

## Top 3 Favorite Characters
1. Eleven
2. Dustin Henderson
3. Steve Harrington
